Upon the first bite, the palate encounters the vibrant, tart acidity of wild mountain blueberries simmered down to a velvety reduction. This quickly surrenders to the dense, opulent creaminess of slow-baked curd cheese, grounded by the subtle roasted spice and toasted butter snap of stone-ground digestive crumb.
