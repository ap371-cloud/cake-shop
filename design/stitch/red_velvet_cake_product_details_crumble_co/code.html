Artisan Technique

Slow Whipped Velveteen Frosting

Standard commercial red velvet recipes lean on powdered sugar and synthetic shortening. At Crumble & Co., we whip pure block Philadelphia cream cheese with cultured churned butter and organic Bourbon vanilla extract at cold room temperatures.

The result is a delicate, tangy counterbalance that allows the natural subtle earthiness of Dutch-processed cocoa and wholesome buttermilk sponge to breathe without overwhelming sweetness.

egg Buttermilk Sponge

Cultured dairy buttermilk, heirloom stoneground flour, fine caster sugar, natural unsweetened Dutch cocoa, and cold-pressed sunflower oil.

water_drop Cream Cheese Glaze

Authentic Philadelphia cream cheese (pasteurized milk & cream), churned sweet cream butter, pure icing cane sugar, fresh citrus squeeze.

grade Aroma & Natural Pigment

Madagascar Bourbon vanilla bean pods, roasted cocoa husk essence, and botanical organic red beetroot extract.

warning Allergen Notice

Contains Dairy (cream cheese, butter, buttermilk) and Gluten (wheat). Prepared in our craft atelier where tree nuts (almonds, pistachios, hazelnuts) and soy are handled. Please specify nut sensitivities at order.

kitchen Care & Chilling Instructions

Store refrigerated between 2°C – 5°C. For optimal melt-in-mouth mouthfeel, remove from the refrigerator 15 minutes before serving so the cream cheese reaches room temperature serenity. Consume within 72 hours.
