Sensory Profile

Our signature Chocolate Truffle Cake honors classical French patisserie traditions. Anchored by a 70% dark Valrhona cacao profile, each bite balances a deep, earthy bittersweet note against velvety ganache infused with organic vanilla bean from southern orchards. The sponge retains an extraordinarily moist, tight crumb structure that effortlessly melts alongside chilled tea or espresso.

Unhurried, Natural Ingredients

We use no synthetic premixes, palm oil, or artificial stabilizers. Every cake starts from raw, unadulterated kitchen essentials:

  • eco Heirloom stoneground flour (Maida-free options available)
  • eco 70% Single-Origin Belgian Dark Chocolate Couverture
  • eco Small-batch slow-churned cultured dairy butter
  • eco Madagascan bourbon vanilla extract & raw cane sugar

Allergen & Kitchen Advisory

Contains: Dairy, Wheat (Gluten), and Soy Lecithin (natural chocolate emulsifier).

Egg Status: Formulated by default in dedicated eggless batches with yogurt and condensed milk emulsion. Standard egg-based recipe baked in separated atelier line upon request.

Nut Warning: Crafted in an artisan atelier that also processes pistachios, almonds, hazelnuts, and walnuts.
