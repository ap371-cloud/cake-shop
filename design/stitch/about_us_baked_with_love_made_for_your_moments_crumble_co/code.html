history_edu Our Story & Philosophy

Rejecting the Shortcuts,
Honoring the Slow Craft.

Crumble & Co. began in 2018 not inside a commercial factory, but around a small stone countertop in an sun-drenched quarter kitchen. We were exhausted by industrial bakeries masking dull flour with powdered shortenings, synthetic syrups, and high-fructose pastes.

We chose the quiet path of the classical French atelier: slow 36-hour sourdough cold fermentation, stone-ground heirloom wheats milled locally, and real cultured cream butter with a minimum of 84% butterfat. Every bake you hold has been mixed, rested, laminated, and shaped by hands that take immense pride in the crust and the crumb.

“A great cake doesn't simply decorate an occasion—it crystallises it in collective memory.”
